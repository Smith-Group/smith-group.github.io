Tools for Kinetic Ensemble analysis of biomolecular structures using
nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) observables. Reads multistate
coordinate ensembles and 'PDB' trajectories, detects equivalent proton
permutations, constructs transition-rate and spectral-density data
structures, and back-calculates dipole-dipole interaction tensors,
cross-relaxation rates, and heteronuclear relaxation rates such as
longitudinal (R1), transverse (R2), and nuclear Overhauser effect (NOE)
data. Also provides utilities for preparing, reading, and writing
restraint tables and for evaluating restraint energies from coordinates
or trajectories. Methods are described in Smith et al. (2020)
\<[doi:10.1007/s10858-019-00288-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10858-019-00288-8)\>
and Alhossary and Smith (2026)
\<[doi:10.1021/acs.jpcb.5c08554](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.5c08554)\>.
